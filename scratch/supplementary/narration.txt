1:53.60–2:35.82  Scaling and ablations

First, look at the full method in yellow: binary task rewards with beta sampling. Evaluated success rises at every tested scale, reaching about ninety-seven percent with two hundred and fifty-six thousand parallel environments. Now add rewards for reaching and alignment, keeping beta sampling. Success at the largest scale drops by about twenty percentage points. The gap shows that more detailed rewards do not necessarily make this task easier to learn. Return to the full method, and change only the sampling rule.
